CFD Investigation • Jul 2026–Present

Aerospike vs. Convergent-Divergent Nozzles

Comparative compressible gas dynamics study evaluating altitude compensation mechanisms between an annular aerospike and a conventional Bell CD nozzle.

  • MOC Supersonic Profiling: Traced inviscid expansion contours using Method of Characteristics coordinates for both nozzle geometries.
  • Matched Throat Area: Enforced identical throat area (At = 314.16 mm²) to guarantee equal mass flow rates at P₀ = 2 MPa and T₀ = 300 K.
  • Multi-Altitude Matrix: Solved 2D axisymmetric density-based Navier-Stokes equations with k-ω SST across 0 km (1.013 bar), 5 km (0.540 bar), 10 km (0.265 bar), and 15 km (0.121 bar), demonstrating that the aerospike eliminates severe over-expansion shock separation.

PBL Project • Apr–Jun 2026

Dassault Rafale Flight Performance Envelope

Analytical mission-performance sizing paired with an exploratory 3D viscous CFD investigation on a coupled delta-canard fighter airframe.

  • Analytical Mission Polar: Sized baseline geometry (S = 45.6 m², AR = 2.60) to model zero-lift drag (CD0 = 0.02) and induced drag factor (K = 0.122) across operational flight regimes.
  • Performance Limits: Determined maximum aerodynamic efficiency (L/D)max ≈ 9.30 at 175 m/s, maximum unpowered glide range > 102 km from 11 km cruise, and peak rate of climb (R/C) > 93 m/s.
  • Power Curve Intersection: Located level-flight maximum velocity (Vmax ≈ 515 m/s) where Power Required intersects Power Available (Pavail = T × V).
  • Viscous CFD Exploration: Evaluated canard vortex shedding and downwash over the delta wing in ANSYS Fluent under a 512k student cell constraint.

Aerostructures • Apr 2026–Present

Tapered Fighter Wing Structural Analysis

Internal structural sizing, static bending compliance, and modal dynamic analysis of a 10.0 m semi-span wing box using aerospace-grade alloy properties.

  • Internal Framework: Designed front and rear I-beam spars, 13 transverse ribs with weight-reduction lightening holes, and spanwise skin stringers in Aluminum 7075-T6 (Fty = 503 MPa, E = 71.7 GPa).
  • Static Load Deflection: Applied a 30 kN upward flight load with cantilever root support, resolving an elastic tip deflection of 0.295 m (2.95% of semi-span) and peak von Mises stress of 79.89 MPa (< 16% of yield).
  • Modal Flutter Separation: Extracted the first 6 natural frequencies, identifying Mode 1 out-of-plane bending (1.49 Hz), Mode 2 second bending (4.87 Hz), and Mode 3 torsion (6.18 Hz).
