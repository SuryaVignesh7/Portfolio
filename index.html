Aerospike vs. Convergent-Divergent Nozzles

Aerostructures • Apr 2026–Present

Tapered Fighter Wing Structural Analysis

Internal structural sizing, static bending compliance, and modal dynamic analysis of a 10.0 m semi-span wing box using aerospace-grade alloy properties.

  • Internal Framework: Designed front and rear I-beam spars, 13 transverse ribs with weight-reduction lightening holes, and spanwise skin stringers in Aluminum 7075-T6 (Fty = 503 MPa, E = 71.7 GPa).
  • Static Load Deflection: Applied a 30 kN upward flight load with cantilever root support, resolving an elastic tip deflection of 0.295 m (2.95% of semi-span) and peak von Mises stress of 79.89 MPa (< 16% of yield).
  • Modal Flutter Separation: Extracted the first 6 natural frequencies, identifying Mode 1 out-of-plane bending (1.49 Hz), Mode 2 second bending (4.87 Hz), and Mode 3 torsion (6.18 Hz).

LEO Constellation Mission Trade Study

Parametric orbital study comparing circular LEO regimes (500, 600, and 700 km) for optical Earth observation and regional target access.

  • Payload & Grid Sizing: Defined a nadir-pointing sensor with a 20.0° half-cone angle evaluated over a 1,008-point global grid (−60° to +70° latitude) and a target asset at Nagpur, India.
